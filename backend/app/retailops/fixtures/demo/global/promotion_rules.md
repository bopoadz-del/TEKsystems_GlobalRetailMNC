# Store Operations — Spring Promo 2024 Promotion Display Rules (Global Region)

These rules govern in-store execution of the Spring Promo 2024 promotion. All data is
fictional and for demonstration only.

- The Spring Promo endcap must display approved Spring Promo signage at all times.
- Promotional price cards must be visible on the shelf edge for every promoted SKU.
- Dates and unit pricing on price cards must match the approved promotion sheet.
- Restricted items (tobacco, alcohol) must not appear in any Spring Promo promotion.
- Chilled promotional stock must be rotated so nearest expiry is front-facing.
- The endcap planogram must be photographed at store open and uploaded daily.
