# Meridian Markets — Spring Fresh 2024 Promotion Display Rules (Global Region)

These rules govern in-store execution of the Spring Fresh 2024 promotion at
international flagship stores. All data is fictional and for demonstration only.

- The Spring Fresh endcap must display approved campaign signage at all times.
- Promotional price cards must be visible on the shelf edge for every promoted SKU.
- Dates and unit pricing on price cards must match the approved promotion sheet.
- Restricted items (tobacco, alcohol) must not appear in any Spring Fresh promotion.
- Chilled promotional stock must be rotated so nearest expiry is front-facing.
- The endcap planogram must be photographed at store open and uploaded daily.
