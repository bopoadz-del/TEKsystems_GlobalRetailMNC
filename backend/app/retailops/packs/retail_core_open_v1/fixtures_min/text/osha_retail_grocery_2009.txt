# Guidelines for Retail Grocery Stores — OSHA (fixture excerpt)

## Checkout and Bagging
Use adjustable workstations and anti-fatigue mats to reduce musculoskeletal risk.

## Stocking
Train associates on safe lifting for cases and totes; avoid overhead stacking beyond reach.

## Bakery Meat and Deli Produce
Rotate tasks and provide sharp cutting tools with cut-resistant gloves where appropriate.

## Training
Provide workplace-risk identification and storewide ergonomic solutions training.
