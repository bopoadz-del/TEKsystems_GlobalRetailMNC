# Advertising FAQs: A Guide for Small Business — FTC (fixture excerpt)

## Truthful Advertising
Advertising must be truthful and non-deceptive. Claims require a reasonable basis
in evidence before they are made.

## Price and Product Claims
Material product and price claims must not omit important information that would
mislead consumers.

## Bait and Switch
Bait-and-switch advertising practices are unlawful. Catalogues share responsibility
for claims they disseminate.
