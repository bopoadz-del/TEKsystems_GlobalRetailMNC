# FDA Food Code 2022 — Excerpt (fixture for offline pilot)

## Receiving and Storage
Time/temperature control for safety food shall be received at required temperatures.
Refrigerated food must be maintained at 5°C (41°F) or less during storage.

## Temperature Control
Hot holding of time/temperature control for safety food shall be at 57°C (135°F) or above.
Cold holding shall be at 5°C (41°F) or less.

## Date Marking
Ready-to-eat time/temperature control for safety food prepared and held refrigerated
for more than 24 hours shall be clearly marked with the dispose-by date.

## Hygiene
Food employees shall clean hands and exposed portions of arms prior to food preparation.
